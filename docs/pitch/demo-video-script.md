# Demo video script: "Bored of the grid?"

A pitch video of about 75 seconds for LinkedIn, Instagram Reels or TikTok. The voiceover is
casual and a little fed up at first, then it warms up as the app takes over the screen. Every shot
below is a screen that already exists in the app. Record at 1440×900 for widescreen, or at
390×844 for a vertical cut.

> Before recording: the closing lines ("free", "sign up today", "tell your friends") are only
> true once real accounts and `/@username` links ship (roadmap P3). Until then, end on "coming
> soon" or a waitlist link.

## 1. The problem (0:00–0:10)

**On screen:** a fast, grey, blurred scroll of a generic photo grid and feed. Cut it
desaturated.

**VO:** "Be honest. Aren't you a little bored of this? The same square grid. The same feed. Your
art squeezed into a thumbnail, between ads and someone's lunch."

## 2. The turn (0:10–0:16)

**On screen:** hard cut to the ourspace gateway. The colour comes back.

**VO:** "What if your page felt like _you_, and not like a template?"

## 3. Make yourself (0:16–0:26)

**On screen:** the onboarding avatar builder. Tap through hair, skin and outfit colours. The
low-poly avatar spins on its plinth.

**VO:** "On ourspace, you start by building your own little low-poly self. No selfies needed."

## 4. Your page, your way (0:26–0:38)

**On screen:** scroll slowly down a creator's profile: the avatar top left, About Me, then a
photos module, a music module and a moodboard. Add or reorder a module on camera.

**VO:** "Then your page builds itself around what you actually make. Paint _and_ make beats? Stack
both. Instead of a static grid of pictures, people get your work, your sound and your taste,
all in one place."

## 5. Step inside (0:38–0:52)

**On screen:** tap a photo module and walk into the gallery room in third person. Pass the framed
works under the picture lights, and stop on a wall label.

**VO:** "And this is the part no grid can do. Your photos aren't a grid any more. They're a room.
Your friends can literally walk through your work."

**Optional beat:** cut to the rave room (the LED floor, the mirror ball, the DJ's name in neon).
"Make music? Your page can be a club."

## 6. Go visiting (0:52–1:04)

**On screen:** the orbit launch. The avatar runs across the moodboard ledge, leaps, gets caught
by the ship, and the planets drift in. Select a planet and open another creator's profile.

**VO:** "Want to find new people? Don't doomscroll. Fly. Every planet is a person. Land on
someone's page and see what they've been making."

## 7. The ask (1:04–1:15)

**On screen:** pull back to your own profile, avatar waving. End card: the ourspace★ logo and the
URL.

**VO:** "It's free. Make your page, send it to your friends, and build out your worlds together.
Keep everyone updated on your art and your music, without the feed. ourspace. Come build yours."

## Production notes

- **Music:** start with a flat, muted loop under the "bored" section. Switch to something warmer
  and more lo-fi on the hard cut to the gateway.
- **Capture:** record the launch and the room walk in one take each. They're built with no hard
  cuts, so let them breathe.
- **Text overlays:** keep them short and few. Use the app's own pixel and serif fonts, not stock
  captions.
- **Placeholders to fill:** the final URL, and the product name (_ourspace_ or the working title
  _Atrium_; check the trademark first).
